Once a valid refund request has been reviewed, verified and approved, the refund will generally be **initiated within 7 working days**.

For the purpose of this Refund Policy, “Working Days” generally means Monday to Friday, excluding applicable public holidays.

Please note that the time required for the refunded amount to appear in your account may depend upon:

- Your bank
- Credit or debit card issuer
- Payment gateway
- EMI provider
- BNPL provider
- Financing partner
- Other third-party payment provider

Any delay caused by a bank, payment provider, lender or third-party financing partner after we have successfully initiated the refund will be subject to that provider's policies and processing timelines.

- Pay Later

- Bank
